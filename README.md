CONTROLS:
W to go up
S to go down
click and drag the cat to shoot (cat travels to the direction of the arrow)
each cat costs differently, calculated in cats. the cost is 1/2/2/3/3

CATS:
pop cat: 1 bounce, 1 cost, the standard cat
happy cat: multiple bounces, 2 cost.
grumpy cat: 1 bounce, 2 cost, but explodes on contact with a dog
nyan cat: 0 bounces, 3 cost, but ignores gravity
long cat: 1 bounce, 3 cost, long :)
